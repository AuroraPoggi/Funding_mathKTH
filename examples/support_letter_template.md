# Example: Support Letter for a Travel Scholarship

This is a real support letter, anonymized, used successfully in a travel
scholarship application. Share the structure with your supervisor if they're
not sure what to write — it only takes them a few minutes.

---

Support letter for travel scholarship,

I strongly recommend that **[Student Name]** is given the opportunity to
attend the **[Conference Name and acronym]** conference that takes place
**[dates]** in **[location]**. The **[acronym]** is a series of international
conferences that [one line on why this conference series matters in the
field].

[Student Name] is pursuing their doctoral studies under my guidance at the
Department of Mathematics at KTH – Royal Institute of Technology, Stockholm.
Their research covers [list 2–4 relevant topics, e.g. probability theory,
machine learning, dynamical systems, inverse problems]. [One sentence on how
this area connects to the conference's field.] The upcoming conference offers
a unique opportunity for [Student Name] to get acquainted with specialists in
these areas at a crucial phase of their PhD. Participation will also offer
them the opportunity to showcase their work and gain insight into recent
advancements in the field. Additionally, it will give [Student Name] the
opportunity to expand their academic and professional network, which is
essential for future collaborations and a possible research period abroad.

Best regards,

[Supervisor Name, title, affiliation]

---

**Tips for using this template:**
- Keep it short — one paragraph of introduction/context and one paragraph on
  why *this specific* conference matters for *this specific* student is
  usually enough.
- Ask your supervisor early; most funding calls require the letter to be
  signed, so leave a few days of buffer before the deadline.
- If the call has a specific format or required statements (e.g. "the
  department confirms no other funding is available"), flag that to your
  supervisor so they can include it.

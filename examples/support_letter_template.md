# Example: Support Letter for a Travel Scholarship
---
Support letter for travel scholarship,

I strongly recommend that **[Student Name]** is given the opportunity to
attend the **[Conference Name and acronym]** conference that takes place
**[dates]** in **[location]**. The **[acronym]** is a series of international
conferences that [one line on why this conference series matters in the
field].

[Student Name] is pursuing their doctoral studies under my guidance at the
Department of Mathematics at KTH – Royal Institute of Technology, Stockholm.
[Her/His] research covers [list 2–4 relevant topics]. [One sentence on how
this area connects to the conference's field.] The upcoming conference offers
a unique opportunity for [Student Name] to get acquainted with specialists in
these areas at a crucial phase of [her/his] PhD. Participation will also offer
[Student Name] the opportunity to showcase their work and gain insight into recent
advancements in the field. Additionally, it will give [Student Name] the
opportunity to expand their academic and professional network, which is
essential for future collaborations and a possible research period abroad.

Best regards,

[Supervisor Name, title, affiliation]

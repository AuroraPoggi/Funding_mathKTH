# Funding_mathKTH

A survival guide to getting your conference travel paid for, written by a
PhD student at the KTH Math department who has filled out one too many
budget spreadsheets so you don't have to start from scratch.

First things first: **do you even have money to spend?** Ask your
supervisor whether you have your own project number or you're borrowing
theirs, then email `finance@math.kth.se` and ask how much is left. 

## Where to apply for travel funding

- **Erasmus staff mobility (Europe):**
  You can get this one twice over your whole PhD, up to a month each time
  (generous). Best part: if you don't spend it all, no one asks
  for receipts, so you can use the leftovers for future trips. See
  https://intra.kth.se/en/anstallning/karriar/karriar-och-kompetensutveckling-utbud/kategori/personalmobilitet/personalmobilitet-inom-europa-via-erasmus-1.30170
  Contact **Anna Hultgren** at **annahg@kth.se** — she's lovely and replies super fast.

- **KTH scholarship announcements:**
  https://www.kth.se/en/student/stod/studier/stipendier/stipendieutlysningar
  Note: some require a Master degree at KTH. 

- **Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences (KVA):**
  https://www.kva.se/en/scholarship/general-announcement-mathematics/
  

- **Swedish Mathematical Society announcements:**
  https://www.swe-math-soc.se/aktuellt/aktuellt.html
  Grants are often small (won't cover a long flight on their own),
  but better than nothing.

## Typical documents requested

Every call is a little different, but here what I was asked usually:

- **CV**
- **Travel budget** — a line-by-line breakdown of what the trip costs,
  usually including:
  - Activity (conference/workshop name)
  - Registration number (*diarienummer*) of the announcement
  - Currency and amount in original currency
  - Exchange rate and total amount in SEK
  - Notes and itemized costs, for example:
    - Conference fee (state if accommodation/meals are included)
    - Flight tickets (can be based on a Big Travel quote and screenshot)
    - Roundtrip to/from the airport in Sweden
    - Airport transfers abroad (roundtrip)
    - Local travel costs abroad
    - Visa costs
    - Accommodation
- **Support letter for travel scholarship**, signed by your supervisor —
  gently remind them early, letters apparently don't write themselves.

# Funding_mathKTH
How to get travel funding as a PhD student at KTH math department

But first of all, how to know if you have funding left, first ask your supervisor whether you have your own project number or you rely on their own, then you can always ask finance@math.kth.se to tell you how much money are left. 


## Where to apply for travel funding

- **Erasmus staff mobility (Europe):**
  You can get this type of funding 2 times over your PhD and each time up to a month (but it is generous) so you might stay longer with that money. Also is super good as if you do not spend all the money you can use them for future travelling as they do not ask for receipts or anything:), see
  https://intra.kth.se/en/anstallning/karriar/karriar-och-kompetensutveckling-utbud/kategori/personalmobilitet/personalmobilitet-inom-europa-via-erasmus-1.30170  
  Contact **Anna Hultgren** at **annahg@kth.se**. She is very kind and usually replies quickly.

- **KTH scholarship announcements:**  
  https://www.kth.se/en/student/stod/studier/stipendier/stipendieutlysningar  
  Note: some scholarships require that you completed your Master's studies at KTH.

- **Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences (KVA):**  
  https://www.kva.se/en/scholarship/general-announcement-mathematics/  
  These may be less generous depending on the call.

- **Swedish Mathematical Society announcements:**  
  https://www.swe-math-soc.se/aktuellt/aktuellt.html  
  Many grants are relatively small (often not enough for long trips), but still worth applying for.

## Typical documents requested

Depending on the funding source, required documents can vary. In general, prepare:

- **CV**
- **Travel budget**, usually including:
  - Activity (conference/workshop name) 
  - Registration number (*diarienummer*) of the announcement
  - Currency and amount in original currency
  - Exchange rate and total amount in SEK
  - Notes and itemized costs, for example:
    - Conference fee (state if accommodation/meals are included)
    - Flight tickets (can be based on Big Travel quote and screenshot)
    - Roundtrip to/from airport in Sweden
    - Airport transfers abroad (roundtrip)
    - Local travel costs abroad
    - Visa costs
    - Accommodation
- **Support letter for travel scholarship**, signed by your supervisor

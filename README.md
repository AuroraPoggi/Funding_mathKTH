# Funding at math Department KTH for PhDs

Where to look for travel funding as a PhD student in the math department. 
This repo contains all the scholarships and funding sources I found over my 4 years as a PhD student at KTH—if you know of others, please share, and I'll add them!

First: **Are you sure you do not have money to spend?** Ask your
supervisor, whether you have your own project number or you're using
theirs, then email `finance@math.kth.se` and ask how much is left. 

## Where to apply for travel funding

- **Erasmus staff mobility (Europe):** You can get this one twice over your whole PhD, up to a month each time.
  (generous). The best part is that if you don't spend it all, no one asks for receipts, so you can use the leftovers for future trips. See https://intra.kth.se/en/anstallning/karriar/karriar-och-kompetensutveckling-utbud/kategori/personalmobilitet/personalmobilitet-inom-europa-via-erasmus-1.30170
Contact **Anna Hultgren** at **annahg@kth.se** — she's lovely and replies super fast.
- 

- **KTH scholarship announcements:**
  https://www.kth.se/en/student/stod/studier/stipendier/stipendieutlysningar
Some require a master’s degree at KTH. 

- **Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences (KVA):**
  https://www.kva.se/en/scholarship/general-announcement-mathematics/
  

- **Swedish Mathematical Society announcements:**
  https://www.swe-math-soc.se/aktuellt/aktuellt.html
Grants are often not too generous and won't cover a long flight on their own, but better than nothing.

## Typical documents requested

Every call is a little different, but here’s what I was usually asked:

- **CV**
- **Travel budget** 
- **Support letter for travel scholarship**, signed by your supervisor
